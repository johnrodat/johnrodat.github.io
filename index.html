Assumed Identity

A profile of Trevanian — five-million-copy bestselling author, deliberate phantom, and former resident of a forgotten block of North Pearl Street, Albany. Traces the man behind the pseudonym from the city's margins to worldwide literary celebrity and back into silence.

The Best Intelligencer

Six years. 150-plus installments. A deadpan satirical column that never once explained the joke. The excerpt below — "Wet Dream Police" — is delivered entirely in the voice of a fictional right-wing foundation's internal strategy meeting.

"We here at the Foundation for American Rectitude Triumphant are well aware that your time is valuable. Why, in the time that it took Jerry to adjust the height on that Aeron chair, his company made more than the GNP of all but three First World nations. So, he's leaving the tip today!"

Short-Form Sitcom  ·  Writer  ·  ~2013–2014

Barker

A short-form sitcom about an irascible Gen X terrestrial-radio DJ groping his way toward an understanding of recent modernity — written by Rodat and starring John Ross Bowie (The Big Bang Theory, Speechless, Children's Hospital). All episodes written by Rodat, along with an unfilmed traditional half-hour pilot. The short-form videos were produced as a pitch to networks and streaming services. About

I'm a writer and communicator based in Schenectady, NY, with a parallel career in wine and spirits that has given me deep knowledge of a world most writers don't know from the inside. The two tracks converge more than they diverge: both are fundamentally about making something complex feel worth caring about to someone who didn't already know they cared.

I've written features, criticism, and a long-running deadpan column for alt-newsweeklies. I've written brand voice, web copy, and thought-leadership content for private clients. I've scripted animation and live-action comedy, hosted a video series, drawn and animated PSA concepts by hand, and written a novel. I hold a certificate in voiceover from Voice Coaches and have a home studio and mobile production kit. I've been performing improv long enough that showing up on camera doesn't feel like showing up on camera.

Currently available for agency, in-house, or consulting engagements.
