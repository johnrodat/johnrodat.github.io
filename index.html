ABOUT I'm a writer and communicator based in Schenectady, NY, with a parallel career in wine and spirits that has given me deep knowledge of a world most writers don't know from the inside. The two tracks converge more than they diverge: both are fundamentally about making something complex feel worth caring about to someone who didn't already know they cared. I've written features, criticism, and a long-running deadpan column for alt-newsweeklies. I've written brand voice, web copy, and thought-leadership content for private clients. I've scripted animation and live-action comedy, hosted a video series, drawn and animated PSA concepts by hand, and written a novel. I hold a certificate in voiceover from Voice Coaches and have a home studio and mobile production kit. I've been performing improv long enough that showing up on camera doesn't feel like showing up on camera. Currently available for agency, in-house, or consulting engagements.
